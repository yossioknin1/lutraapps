Privacy Policy – Pizza Rush: Slice to Empire

Developer: LutraApps · Package: com.pizzarush.app · Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy explains what information the Pizza Rush: Slice to Empire Android app ("the app") handles. The app does not require an account, and we do not run servers that receive your data.

Information that stays on your device

Game progress and settings are stored only on your device. The game has no account, chat or online play.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads served by Google AdMob (a banner on menu and pause screens, an occasional full-screen ad after completing a stage, and optional rewarded ads you choose to watch). To serve and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect:

DataPurpose
Device identifiers, including the Android advertising IDAdvertising, fraud prevention
App interactions (for example ad views and taps)Advertising, analytics
Diagnostics (crash and performance data from the ads SDK)Diagnostics
Approximate location derived from IP addressAdvertising

This data is collected by Google, encrypted in transit, and handled under Google's Privacy Policy. See also how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent through Google's User Messaging Platform before personalized ads are shown, and you can change that choice later in the app. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in Android Settings › Google › Ads (or Settings › Privacy › Ads).

Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, files, messages or precise location. The app does not sell personal information.

Permissions

Internet access (to load ads), and the advertising ID permission added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

All app data lives on your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes it. Advertising data held by Google can be managed through your Google account and device ad settings.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest revision.
